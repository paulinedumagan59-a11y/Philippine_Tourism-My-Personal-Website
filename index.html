⛰️Chocolate Hills

Chocolate Hills is a famous place in Bohol, located in Carmen. it has over 1,260 to 1,776 cone-shaped mounds in Bohol, Philippines. They are covered in green grass that turns brown in the dry season. This brown color makes them look like giant chocolate drops.

😌 My Favorite Destination

My favorite Bohol destination is CHOCOLATE HILLS because it is very relaxing to see mountains and enjoying its fresh air, I had visited this many times and I really love this place.
